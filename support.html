開始使用

Get started

  1. 首次開啟時，先閱讀安全說明。App 不會自動啟動感測器或發送訊號。
  2. 從首頁選擇 SOS、接收器、求救資料卡或訓練。
  3. 發送前確認輸出方式、摩斯內容、速度與時間上限。
  4. 緊急狀況下，有正式通訊管道時請優先使用。
  1. Read the safety notice on first launch. The app never starts sensors or a transmission automatically.
  2. Choose SOS, Receivers, Emergency Card, or Training from the home screen.
  3. Before transmitting, confirm the output, Morse content, speed, and time limit.
  4. In an emergency, use official communication channels first whenever available.

行動網路壅塞、降速或中斷時

When mobile networks are limited

行動數據無法使用時，語音與簡訊仍可能可用。實際狀況依災情、位置與電信網路而異；請先嘗試 110、119、112、語音、簡訊及其他正式緊急通訊方式。

Voice calls and SMS may remain available when mobile data does not. Availability depends on the incident, location, and carrier. Try emergency numbers, voice, SMS, and other official channels first.

事前準備 Prepare beforehand

從首頁開啟「通訊受限應變」，保存官方避難資訊、確認家庭集合點、完成雙 QR 配對、測試附近家庭傳送，並準備現金、實體票證與行動電源。

Open Communication Fallback from Home. Save official shelter information, confirm the family meeting point, complete two-QR pairing, test nearby transfer, and prepare cash, physical tickets, and power.

附近家庭傳送 Nearby family transfer

已配對且位於近距離的裝置，可在 MorseAid 保持前景時直接交換加密現況；不需要網際網路，但藍牙與本機無線連線能力必須可用。

Previously paired devices at close range can exchange encrypted statuses while MorseAid remains in the foreground. Internet is not required, but local Bluetooth and wireless connectivity must be available.

使用簡訊版現況卡

Use an SMS status card

  1. 先在家庭安全站記錄自己的現況。
  2. 在待送匣或已送現況中選擇「簡訊版現況卡」。
  3. 檢查姓名、狀態、人數、時間與備註。精確座標預設不包含；只有可信任的收件人才應手動加入。
  4. iPhone 可開啟系統簡訊編輯器；無法直接傳簡訊的 iPad 可複製文字或使用系統分享。
  5. 自行選擇收件人並按下傳送，要求對方以簡訊回覆 OK。
  1. Record your status in Family Safety Station.
  2. Choose “SMS status card” from an outbox or sent status.
  3. Review the name, status, people count, time, and note. Precise coordinates are excluded by default; include them only for a trusted recipient.
  4. On iPhone, open the system SMS composer. On an iPad that cannot send SMS directly, copy the text or use system share.
  5. Choose the recipient and send it yourself. Ask the recipient to reply OK by SMS.

重要：簡訊現況卡是未加密純文字。不要加入醫療紀錄、證件號碼、密碼或其他敏感資訊。關閉簡訊編輯器不代表訊息已送達或家人已閱讀。

Important: An SMS status card is unencrypted plain text. Do not include medical records, ID numbers, passwords, or other sensitive information. Closing the composer does not prove delivery or that family read the message.

選擇一組測試訊息，再以 MorseAid 的聲音或光線接收器辨識。測試完全在瀏覽器中執行，不會存取麥克風、相機或網路服務。

Choose a test message, then decode it with MorseAid’s audio or optical receiver. The test runs entirely in your browser and does not access a microphone, camera, or network service.

Every signal requires a manual start and can be stopped at any time. It stops automatically if you switch tabs or leave this page.

改善聲音與影像接收

Improve audio and optical reception

聲音接收 Audio receiver

靠近穩定且清楚的音源，避開語音、音樂、風聲與回音。先在安靜環境校準，再逐步調整門檻。

Move near a stable tone and away from speech, music, wind, and echo. Calibrate in a quiet place before adjusting thresholds.

影像接收 Optical receiver

讓訊號光源保持在中央偵測區，固定手機，避免自動曝光變化、反光與其他閃爍光源。

Keep the light inside the center detection area, steady the phone, and avoid reflections, exposure shifts, or competing flicker.

辨識結果僅供輔助。請比對原始點劃節奏，採取行動前務必人工確認。

Recognition is assistive only. Compare the raw dot-dash rhythm and confirm it manually before acting.

新手課程與 30 天 Pro 計畫

Beginner lessons and the 30-day Pro plan

免費：12 課循序學習 Free: 12 guided lessons

從 SOS 與常用字母開始，透過字母查詢、聲音、閃光、辨識及回想練習建立基礎。摩斯字典也能在列表直接播放，適合快速複習。

Start with SOS and useful letters, then build recall with reference, audio, flashing light, recognition, and practice. Play entries directly from the Morse reference list for quick review.

Pro：30 天自適應路徑 Pro: 30-day adaptive path

每天提供明確目標與弱項重點，依序進行聲音基礎、回想與發報、防災訊號及情境實戰，並記錄連續天數與 3／7／14／30 日里程碑。

Get a daily goal and weak-character focus across four phases: sound foundations, recall and keying, preparedness signals, and field scenarios, with streaks and 3/7/14/30-day milestones.

Koch 是什麼？

What is the Koch method?

Koch 法先用接近實際速度練少量字元，熟練後才逐步加入新字元，避免只靠慢速數點與劃。MorseAid 會依程度逐步解鎖字元。

The Koch method starts with a small character set at near-practical character speed, adding new characters after mastery so you learn rhythm instead of counting dots and dashes.

Farnsworth 節奏是什麼？

What is Farnsworth timing?

字元本身保持清楚的正常節奏，但把字元與單字之間的間隔拉長，讓新手有時間辨識。進步後再逐漸縮短間隔。

Characters keep a clear, practical rhythm while extra spacing between characters and words gives beginners time to recognize them. Spacing decreases as skill improves.

訓練進度只保存在裝置上。啟動每日計畫不會覆蓋你在自由練習中設定的速度、題庫或其他自訂值。

Training progress stays on the device. Starting the daily plan does not overwrite speed, question-set, or other custom practice settings.

家庭安全站、人工交接與附近傳送

Family Safety Station, manual handoff, and nearby transfer

  1. 請在災害前與家人當面完成雙 QR 配對，並口頭核對六位數安全碼。
  2. 記錄現況後，訊息會先進入本機待送匣；這不代表家人已收到。
  3. 選擇「以 QR 或系統分享交接」可顯示大字摘要及加密 QR，或由你選擇「訊息」、AirDrop 等系統目的地。
  4. 收件家人可掃描 QR，或貼上分享訊息內的 MORSEAID1 加密碼。App 會驗證家庭、成員、簽章、期限與重播。
  5. 兩部裝置都保持 App 在前景，按下「尋找附近家庭裝置」，可在沒有網際網路時嘗試直接交換加密現況。首次使用需允許本機網路。
  6. 只有接收裝置完成驗證與儲存並回傳加密回條後，原裝置才顯示「已到達裝置」；這仍不代表家人已閱讀。
  7. 收件人按下確認後會產生加密回條；原傳送者匯入回條後，才會顯示家人已確認。
  1. Before an emergency, pair face to face with both QR codes and compare the six-digit safety code aloud.
  2. A recorded status first enters the local outbox. This does not mean family received it.
  3. Choose “Hand off by QR or system share” to show a readable card and encrypted QR, or select Messages, AirDrop, or another system destination yourself.
  4. A family recipient can scan the QR or paste the MORSEAID1 encrypted code from the shared message. The app verifies family, membership, signature, expiry, and replay.
  5. Keep MorseAid in the foreground on both devices and tap “Find nearby family devices” to attempt a direct encrypted exchange without internet. Local Network permission is required the first time.
  6. The sender shows “Delivered to device” only after the receiving device verifies, saves, and returns an encrypted receipt. This still does not mean a person read it.
  7. After the recipient confirms, the app creates an encrypted receipt. The original sender must import it before the app shows person confirmation.

首發版範圍：一個家庭由本機與一台配對裝置組成。若要更換裝置，請先在成員列向左滑動並移除目前裝置，再重新配對。

First-release scope: A family consists of this device and one paired device. To replace it, swipe left on the current member, remove it, and pair the new device.

重要：「通道已接收」只表示 QR 已人工交接或 iOS 接受分享要求，不代表送達。若系統提供衛星「訊息」，請依 Apple 畫面操作；MorseAid 無法控制或確認衛星傳輸。

Important: “Accepted by transport” means only that a QR was manually handed off or iOS accepted the share request. It is not delivery proof. If Messages via satellite is available, follow Apple’s interface; MorseAid cannot control or confirm satellite transmission.

權限用途

Permission use

麥克風

Microphone

只在你啟動聲音接收器時使用。音訊在記憶體中即時分析，不錄製、不保存、也不上傳。

Used only after you start the audio receiver. Audio is analyzed in memory and is not recorded, saved, or uploaded.

相機

Camera

只在你啟動影像接收器時使用。畫面會轉換為中央區域亮度樣本；不拍照、不錄影、也不上傳。

Used only after you start the optical receiver. Frames become center-region brightness samples; no photo or video is saved or uploaded.

使用 App 期間的位置

Location while using the app

只有你在求救資料卡按下位置按鈕後才會要求。MorseAid 不會要求「永遠允許」，也不會在背景追蹤位置。

Requested only after you tap the location button on the emergency card. MorseAid never asks for Always access or tracks location in the background.

本機網路

Local Network

只在你開始附近家庭傳送時使用，用來尋找並連線到附近已配對的家庭裝置。離開前景後搜尋會停止。

Used only after you start Nearby Family Transfer to find and connect to nearby paired-family devices. Searching stops when the app leaves the foreground.

若要變更權限，請前往 iPhone「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」，或「設定」→「MorseAid」。

To change access, open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security, or Settings → MorseAid.

常見問題

Frequently asked questions

MorseAid 會自動呼叫救援嗎？

Does MorseAid call emergency services?

不會。App 不會撥號、傳送網路求救或通知任何單位。發出 SOS 不代表救援單位已收到。

No. It does not call, send an online alert, or notify responders. Sending SOS does not mean anyone received it.

沒有網路還能使用嗎？

Can I use it without internet?

免費核心可離線使用。購買、恢復購買及首次取得 App Store 商品資訊需要 Apple 服務。

The free core works offline. Buying, restoring, and initially loading App Store product information require Apple services.

為什麼相機或麥克風無法啟動？

Why will the camera or microphone not start?

檢查 iPhone 或 iPad 設定中的權限，確認沒有其他 App 正在使用感測器，完全關閉 MorseAid 後再開啟。仍無法使用時請回報裝置型號與 iOS／iPadOS 版本。

Check permissions, make sure another app is not holding the sensor, and relaunch MorseAid. If it persists, report the device model and iOS or iPadOS version.

QR Code 內容有加密嗎？

Is the emergency-card QR code encrypted?

沒有。任何能看到 QR Code 的相機都可能讀取內容。選填醫療欄位預設排除，展示前請再次檢查。

No. Any camera that can see the code may read it. Optional medical fields are excluded by default; review the card before presenting it.

仍需要協助？

Still need help?

請在 GitHub 支援追蹤器建立問題，並附上 MorseAid 版本、iPhone 或 iPad 型號、iOS／iPadOS 版本、重現步驟與畫面訊息。請勿公開姓名、位置、醫療資料、Apple ID 或交易憑證。

Open a ticket in the GitHub support tracker with the MorseAid version, iPhone or iPad model, OS version, reproduction steps, and visible error. Do not post names, location, medical details, Apple ID information, or purchase receipts.
