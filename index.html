產品原則

Product principles

在網路不可靠時，核心功能仍然可用

Core tools remain available when networks do not

01

離線核心

Offline core

SOS 設定、訊號發送與接收、求救資料卡、自訂訊息及基礎訓練不需要帳號或伺服器。

SOS setup, signaling, receivers, the emergency card, custom messages, and basic training need no account or server.

02

資料留在裝置

Data stays on device

麥克風與相機資料只在記憶體中即時處理；不錄音、不儲存影像，也不上傳。

Microphone and camera input is processed in memory. It is not recorded, saved, or uploaded.

03

安全界線清楚

Clear safety boundaries

每次發送或接收皆由你明確啟動。MorseAid 不會自動聯絡緊急服務，也不保證訊號送達。

You explicitly start every transmission or receiver. MorseAid does not contact emergency services or guarantee delivery.
